Provenance Neural Engine Community Edition

A clinical study flowing from USDM authoring to SDTM datasets through an NGSI-LD broker, on your laptop, with no cloud account. Broker: upstream NEC Scorpio, in-memory profile. Vocabulary: T.O.P. cr-domain. Transform: sdtm.oak. First time here? Run the setup wizard.

1Author the study

A USDM-style study definition (CARDIO-118, phase 2, two arms) becomes cr-domain NGSI-LD entities in the broker: Study, Arms, Endpoint, EligibilityCriteria, VisitDefinitions, Site, Sponsor.


  


  

    
2Project the design to Dataset-JSON

    
The study design in the broker is projected to CDISC SDTM trial-design
       domains (TS, TA, TI, TV) as Dataset-JSON files in the lake. Standards
       are views, not the stored model.

    
    

  


  

    
3Enroll participants and record visits

    
Execution data is written straight through the broker. Every write
       fires the NGSI-LD subscription: the broker POSTs a notification to the
       listener, which triggers the sdtm.oak transform. That hop replaced
       SNS, SQS, and a Lambda.

    
    
    
    

  


  

    
4Watch the pipeline fire

    
The listener's event log: notifications in, sdtm.oak transform runs
       out. SDTM VS and DM land in the lake as CSV and Dataset-JSON.

    
    

  


  

    
5Query the lake

    
DuckDB SQL over the files in the lake. This is the Athena replacement,
       with zero infrastructure.

    
    
    
    

    

  


  

    
6The sprinkle of AI

    
Natural language in, NGSI-LD entity out. Construct proposes entities;
       commit executes the proposal when the authority gate is enabled
       (PNE_ADAPTIVE_EXECUTE). Uses the Anthropic API when a key is
       configured; otherwise a deterministic offline stub.
